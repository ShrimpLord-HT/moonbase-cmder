Battery — Your electricity

Starts at 3, maximum 9. Sunlight generates power, while running the habitat and sending reports consume it.

If the battery reaches 0, you lose.



Oxygen — Your crew's life support

Starts at 3, maximum 3. Saving electricity reduces oxygen safety by 1. Normal operations restore 1.

If oxygen safety reaches 0, you lose.

It's a simplified safety meter, not a physically accurate oxygen model.



Shift — Your mission's progress

You have 3 turns. Each represents a period of sunlight or shadow.

Reports — Your science objective

Starts at 0. You must transmit at least one report before the mission ends. Sending one costs 2 battery.
