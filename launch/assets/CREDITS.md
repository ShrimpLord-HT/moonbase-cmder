# Source media and AI/SI provenance

The public credits are at [credits.html](../../credits.html). Machine-readable
asset origins and AI-assistance scope are in [provenance.json](../../provenance.json).
ChatGPT/Codex assisted with mission development, code, procedural visuals,
dialogue, educational adaptations, simulated scanner readings, documentation,
and tests. The six source JPEGs below were downloaded from their publishers;
no image-generation model was used to create them during this development work.

- `earth-blue-marble.jpg`: Blue Marble 2002, NASA's Earth Observatory; MODIS/Terra composite. Displayed as a distant view of Earth, with presentation framing rather than an ephemeris calculation. https://science.nasa.gov/resource/blue-marble-2002/

These images illustrate fictional game missions; they do not depict an existing Moonbase Commander outpost.

- `moon-color.jpg`: 2025 color map, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit. NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University, adapted from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera imagery. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
- `moon-height.jpg`: LOLA elevation map from the same kit, NASA/GSFC/MIT. Used for visual terrain relief. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
- `mission-outpost.jpg`: NASA illustration of astronauts and equipment at the lunar South Pole, published with “Lunar Living: NASA’s Artemis Base Camp Concept.” Concept art, not a surface photograph. https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/missions/2020/10/28/lunar-living-nasas-artemis-base-camp-concept/
- `mission-crater.jpg`: Shackleton Crater mosaic from the LROC and ShadowCam teams. Images provided by NASA/KARI/ASU. Web-size JPEG re-encoded for delivery; cropped by CSS for the mission card. https://www.nasa.gov/missions/lro/nasa-moon-camera-mosaic-sheds-light-on-lunar-south-pole/
- `mission-orbiter.jpg`: Artist’s illustration of the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. NASA/GSFC. https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/missions/lro/

The intro spins faster than the real Moon and exaggerates terrain shading for readability. The orbit line is decorative, not a spacecraft trajectory prediction. No NASA affiliation or endorsement is implied.

`../../favicon.ico` is a pre-existing diamond project icon. Its creator and
generation history have not been established in this record; team confirmation
is pending. It is not represented here as a verified human-authored asset.

Third-party media retains its original terms; the repository MIT license does
